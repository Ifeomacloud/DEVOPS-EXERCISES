Blueberry Muffin Recipe

Bake the ultimate blueberry muffins for your guests and loved ones. This recipe shows you how to create cafe-style muffins in your own kitchen.

Yield: 10–12 muffins  •  Prep: 15 min  •  Bake: 20–25 min at 180°C (350°F)

Ingredients

  • 100 gfresh blueberries
  • 300 gplain/all-purpose flour
  • 150 ggranulated sugar
  • 1 tspvanilla extract
  • 60 mlvegetable oil
  • 50 gunsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 2large eggs
  • 1 tspbaking powder
  • 1/2 tspbaking soda
  • 1/4 tspsalt
  • 120 mlmilk (or buttermilk)

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C / 350°F. Line a muffin tin with 10–12 paper liners or grease the cups.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, vegetable oil, melted butter, and vanilla until combined.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined — do not overmix.
  5. Fold in the blueberries carefully to avoid crushing them.
  6. Spoon the batter into the prepared muffin cups, filling each about 2/3 full.
  7. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  8. Allow muffins to cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

Tip: Toss blueberries in a tablespoon of flour before folding them into the batter to help prevent them from sinking.

Enjoy warm or at room temperature. Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days, or freeze for longer storage.
